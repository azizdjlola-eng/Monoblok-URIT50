4.4 MSH addressing - PLEASE CONFIRM
  We currently build our reply as:
      MSH-3 = LIS        (Sending Application)
      MSH-4 = (empty)    (Sending Facility)
      MSH-5 = BIOBASE    (Receiving Application - echoed from your MSH-3)
      MSH-6 = BK-280     (Receiving Facility  - echoed from your MSH-4)
  Your Appendix E text says fields 3 and 4 are set by the LIS developer and
  fields 5 and 6 are set by the user in the analyzer software, but the examples
  in the same document show BIOBASE|BK-280 in fields 3 and 4 of the LIS reply.
  If your software validates these fields, please tell us the exact values it
  expects and we will change them in minutes.
